Stop burning money on
Kubernetes.

kube-waste scans your cluster read-only and prints the idle resources (unused volumes, abandoned load balancers, orphaned pods) as estimated dollars per month. No agents. No sidecars. Just a bill.

Procedure

4 steps
  1. 01

    Obtain

    Grab the binary for your platform from the releases page.

    curl -LO https://github.com/pkhamre/kube-waste/releases/latest/download/kube-waste-linux-amd64
  2. 02

    Execute

    Run it against your local kubeconfig. Nothing is installed in the cluster.

    ./kube-waste
  3. 03

    Assess

    Read the waste table and the total at the bottom. One line per idle resource.

    A kind that can't be listed skips only its own detectors; the rest still report.

  4. 04

    Remediate

    Delete what you confirm is unused. The scan only reports; you decide what goes.

    Verify against your own cloud bill before acting on the estimate.
